# Weatherproof case notes

Case goes on the outside of the front door, with the mic facing across the
threshold to reduce wind noise from the prevailing wind against the door.

- Print in PETG or ASA (not PLA). Thick walls, tongue-and-groove lid joint with a gasket, screws not snap-fit.
- Small (2-3 mm) straight mic port, sealed to the mic with a foam/silicone gasket so sound doesn't bounce around inside.
- Cover the port inside with a waterproof acoustic membrane (ePTFE vent, sold as self-adhesive discs).
- Face the port downward or sideways with a small hood; never upward.
- Foam windscreen over the port and a recessed port/short baffle against direct wind.
- Mount the board and mic on foam or rubber standoffs; add a foam pad between case and door to cut vibration and slam noise.
- USB power cable through a cable gland or tight grommet from below, with a drip loop outside.
- Desiccant packet inside; consider conformal coating on the boards.
- Presence sensor: mmWave (LD2410) works through ~2 mm plastic with no metal behind it; a PIR needs an IR-transparent window (Fresnel lens).
